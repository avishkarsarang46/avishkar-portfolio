Who is Avishkar?

Design is more than looks.

I'm Avishkar Sarang, a Graphic Designer and Video Editor based in Pune, India. I enjoy transforming ideas into clean, impactful designs and engaging videos.

My creative work includes social media posts, posters, branding, promotional videos, short-form reels, visual effects and motion graphics. I focus on detail, strong composition and visuals that communicate clearly.

I'm always learning new techniques and exploring creative ways to make every project better.
